- **Individual CLA** — sign this if you contribute on your own behalf.
- **Entity (Corporate) CLA** — sign this if you contribute as part of your work
  for an employer, or your employer owns the IP in your contributions.

How you sign is described at the end (no paperwork — a comment on your first PR).

## How to sign

We use an automated CLA check on pull requests (see
[`CONTRIBUTING.md`](./CONTRIBUTING.md)). The first time you open a PR, the CLA bot
will comment with a one-line statement to confirm — replying with the requested
phrase (e.g. *“I have read the CLA Document and I hereby sign the CLA”*) records
your agreement against your GitHub identity. No scanning, printing, or email
required. Your signature is stored with the project's records.

By signing, you agree to the terms above for all of your past and future
Contributions to GitReward.
